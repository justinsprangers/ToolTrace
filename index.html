ToolTrace

photo of a tool → Gridfinity bin with a pocket

1. Photo

Drag the 4 orange handles onto the corners of the reference rectangle (any order). Use “Extend past it” (mm) if the tool hangs over its edge, e.g. when using grid points on a cutting mat.

2. Trace

Lower “Sensitivity” picks up more (shiny metal, pale parts); raise it if shadows or grid lines get traced. Lower “Brightness weight” to ignore shadows.

3. Bin

Finger notches

In notch mode, click inside the bin to add a notch and click a notch to remove it.

4. Export

Long bins are split into pieces no wider than “Max units per print”; each piece downloads as its own STL. The pocket runs across the joins.

Help: taking a good photo
  • Lay the tool on plain white paper (or a big sheet of poster board). The whole tool must be on the reference rectangle, unless you use “Extend past it”.
  • Shoot straight down from high up and zoom in (2–3×). Wide-angle close-ups make tall parts look bigger.
  • Even light, no hard shadows. Overcast window light or two lamps from opposite sides.
  • Thick tools: the outline is the silhouette from above. Measure the thickness with calipers and use that (plus ~1 mm) for the pocket depth.
  • Check the result: print a slice first (set height units to 1 and depth to 2 mm) to test the fit.
