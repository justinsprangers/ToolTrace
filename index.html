Tips for a good photo
  • Plain white paper is the most reliable reference. Use tabloid paper or poster board for long tools.
  • Shoot straight down from high up with 2–3× zoom. Close wide-angle shots make tall parts look bigger.
  • Even light, no hard shadows. Spread tools out so they don't touch.
  • Several tools in one photo is fine: each becomes its own shape.
